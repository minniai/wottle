# Wottle Design System — Field & Ledger

Version 1.4 · derived from `Wottle UX Audit.dc.html` (sections 04–09); amended 20 September 2026 by spec 048 (the slip, the rail, the live row's beats, rules outside the room, rated only) and 21 September 2026 by spec 050 (ten moves each on one shared clock: the clock in the caption, the moves lane, rows by move, the move beats, the end-early slip; design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/SU3bj2nTT4tEyCiinoEAAB) and 23 September 2026 by spec 068 (the scoreboard, one grid, terracotta and crimson, the capitalised name; source `docs/design_documentation/260922-game-flow/GAME_FLOW_SPEC.md`, its last two amendments), and in Phase B of the same spec (the last-moved tick, one line 2 by precedence, the missed beat and the stakes, announcements, the resign and end-early slips in the canvas's terms, your own outage); and 23 September 2026 by spec 069 (the table: every match begins with both players sitting down, the ready and void slips, the table's scoreboard and ledger, the 3·2·1 from the server's start, a void is never rated; canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/W1emDuJCT6zNAH4h79N3yo); and 24 September 2026 by spec 070 (pages where there is no field: the door and the lobby with the masthead and the line slot, the challenge composer, the leave slip; the sign-in slip retired; same canvas). Binding for every screen and component in Wottle. When this document and the code disagree, the code is wrong.

Wottle is a two-player Icelandic word duel. Its look comes from the game's own material: a printed word-search page, set in a slab serif so Þ, Ð, Æ and Ö carry weight, marked up by two players with two inks. Paper and ink are the system; teal and terracotta are the seats; crimson marks points lost. Nothing else.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **The field is the game.** Nothing competes with it, nothing on it that is not a letter or a state of a letter. What frames it above and below belongs to the two players and to nothing else. One thing may cover it: the **slip** (§5.9), for the moments a player must notice or decide — sitting down at the table (ready), a table that did not fill (void), leaving a live match (leave), resign, end early, match over — and the field fades beneath it (amended 20 September 2026, spec 048; `claim the win` became `end early` with spec 050; ready and void added 23 September 2026, spec 069; leave added and sign in retired 24 September 2026, spec 070: signing in is the door, a page).
2. **Three homes for every fact.** A fact about a letter lives on the field. A fact about one player (name, rating, moves left, total) lives on that player's row of the **scoreboard** during a match (table, starting, live, match over, void), and in that player's bar in the lobby and the queue. A fact about the match lives in the match's own place: the clock is the scoreboard's first row; words, territory and controls are in the ledger. If a proposed element has no home, it is not added (amended 23 September 2026, spec 068).
3. **Show words, not tiles.** Territory is stored per tile and earned per word. Draw the word.
4. **One clock, two counts, one track.** The match has one 5:00 clock, drawn once, as the scoreboard's first row. Each player's ten moves are drawn on their row beneath it, on the same ten-column track, so time left and moves left compare straight down a column and the clock is never drawn twice (amended 23 September 2026, spec 068).
5. **Colour is identity.** Teal is you, terracotta is the opponent, in every match on every device. The system itself speaks only in ink and paper; urgency is weight, never motion and never a hue. Crimson marks a number of points lost and nothing else (spec 068).
6. **Each beat has one signal.** A move has five beats (set, think, commit, reveal, settle); each gets exactly one visual signal — and each is named in the live row's first line (§7, §8). The opponent's reveal is a beat on your field too, but it never takes a pick from you.
7. **One field, three pages** (spec 070, 24 September 2026). The field has one home, the room: the table, the match and the result are its states, and the board never leaves it. Where there is no field there is a **page** (§5.10): the door, the lobby and the profile, each under a masthead with the line slot beneath it, and the rules page, which teaches. A page never draws a field; the room never draws a masthead. The queue is no longer a place: a search is a state of the line slot on every page.
8. **Teach outside the room.** The rules are a page of their own (`/rules`), reached by `how to play ▸` from the door, the lobby's masthead, the final foot and the match `⋯` menu (a new tab, so a match is never interrupted). Nothing in the room teaches (amended 20 September 2026, spec 048; replaces the first-match sentences and `? rules`).
9. **Loss and win are stated once, in the same voice.** No confetti; a lost match is never crimson (crimson is for points lost, §2). The result is stated on the match-over slip (§5.9) and, beneath it, in the ledger; the winner's name in their seat colour, `draw` in ink.

## 2. Colour

Nine values. Nothing else may appear on screen. Seven are the palette; `--opp-text`
exists only because terracotta is not legible as small text; `--err` marks a number
of points lost and nothing else (23 September 2026, spec 068).

| Token        | Value                                                              | Use                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `--paper`    | `#FFFDF7`                                                          | The only background. Field cells, the scoreboard, bars, ledger, page.                                                                                                          |
| `--ink`      | `#0F1A24`                                                          | Letters, numerals, frames, rules that separate objects, the clock's urgent ticks, primary action fill.                                                                        |
| `--rule`     | `#E6E2D6`                                                          | Hairlines inside objects (cell rules, table rows), the empty portion of a track, empty territory.                                                                              |
| `--tint`     | `#F4F1E8`                                                          | Background of the live row and other "current" rows; the clock row's ground under 1:00.                                                                                         |
| `--muted`    | `#5A6572`                                                          | Secondary mono labels, sub-lines, the running clock's ticks. Minimum for text; never lighter (5.7:1 on paper).                                                                 |
| `--you`      | `#147D7A` teal                                                     | Your seat: your letters in scored words, your bands (14% tint), your moves on the track, your total, your square.                                                             |
| `--opp`      | `#B56A4F` burnished terracotta (OKLCH 0.60 0.10 40; 4.0:1 on paper) | The opponent's seat, same uses: letters, bands (14% settled, 30% live), their moves on the track, squares, totals at 24px and above — only at 17px and above, or where it is not text. |
| `--opp-text` | `#A1583D` terracotta, same hue, darker (5.2:1 on paper, 4.7:1 on `--tint`) | The opponent's seat wherever it is **text below 17px**: ledger words, the numeral on a scored letter, lobby scores, the phone scoreboard's 20px total, the profile's best-word names. |
| `--err`      | `#AD1F3D` crimson (OKLCH 0.49 0.18 16; 6.8:1 on paper)             | The number of points lost, and nothing else: the `−5` of `no word` (in the ledger and on the missed beat's line 2), the `−5` of `not played`, and the stakes number in `−15 if unplayed`.                                |

Rules:

- Seat colours are **relative to the viewer** and resolved through one function (`getSeatColors(viewerSlot, slot)`). Never bind a colour to `player_a` / `player_b`.
- Seat colours at full strength for letters, tracks, totals and squares; at **14%** as the band tint of a settled word; at **30%** during a live reveal (`rgba(181,106,79,…)` for terracotta). No other alpha values.
- Terracotta as text below 17px uses `--opp-text`; teal needs no variant (4.9:1 on paper). `getSeatColors` returns both, so no caller decides. Decided 15 September 2026; it replaced two contrast exclusions in the axe suite.
- **Crimson has one door.** It is applied only by the `.points-lost` class, and only `components/room/PointsLost.tsx` applies it. Only the number is crimson; its label (`no word`, `not played`, `if unplayed`) stays `--muted`, so colour is never the only carrier. A floored 0 is `--muted`, not crimson. Crimson never marks a seat, a rating change (`1187 → 1179 · −8` stays ink), urgency, a frame, focus or a control.
- Terracotta is quiet (chroma 0.10, a brown hue at 40°) and sits beside teal as an earth pair. Crimson is cooler and deeper than terracotta, so a `−5` in the opponent's column reads as a loss, not as their colour. Teal and terracotta are close under protanopia, so seats are never told apart by colour alone: the square, the name and the row's place on the scoreboard, and the ledger's column, carry the same fact.
- A scored letter is the colour of the player who froze it first, and stays so when a later word crosses it. No letter is ink for being in two words (spec 049, 20 September 2026; it replaced the shared-letter ink rule of 16 September).
- No gradients, no shadows, no radii, no blur, no other accent. `border-radius` is `0` everywhere and stays there.
- A future move's numeral uses `#B9B4A6`: the ledger's row labels. This is the only exception to the nine values and appears there only (the rail's cells used it until the rail was removed, 21 September 2026). The labels are `aria-hidden` — the scoreboard carries the viewer's count — and are the permitted exclusion from the automated contrast check.

Contrast: `--ink` on `--paper` 16:1; `--muted` on `--paper` 5.7:1; `--you` on `--paper` 4.9:1; `--opp` on `--paper` 4.0:1 (used only at ≥17px or for non-text marks; the desktop scoreboard total is 26px); `--opp-text` 5.2:1; `--err` 6.8:1.

## 3. Type

Two families in the room. A text face may be used for long-form pages outside the game (help, about), never inside the room.

| Token          | Family                                          | Use                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `--font-board` | Zilla Slab (500, 600, 700; latin + latin-ext)   | Every letter on the field (600), names (600), words in the ledger (600, 0.04em tracking), headings (600), the wordmark `Wottle` / `Orðusta` (700). |
| `--font-mono`  | Red Hat Mono (400, 500, 600; latin + latin-ext) | Every numeral: the clock, totals, points, ratings, values. Labels: 11px, 0.12em tracking, uppercase. Tabular numerals on.                         |

Scale (desktop → phone): field letter 55% of cell height; value numeral `max(9px, 18% of cell height)`, hidden entirely below a 32px cell where 18% is unreadable — the cell's `aria-label` still carries the value (decided 15 September 2026); scoreboard name 15 (Zilla 600); scoreboard sub-line 10 (mono, uppercase); scoreboard clock numeral 24 (mono, tabular); scoreboard total 26 → 20; bar name 17 → 15 and bar sub-line 11 → 10 (lobby, queue); ledger words 14; ledger points 12; verdict 20; caption wordmark 16; profile name 28; profile rating 48.

Display tier (spec 068, game flow §8.5), one ladder for every size in the product: 11 label · 13 table numeral · 15 body and names · 17 live row and bar names · 22 title · 28 headline · 40 display-2 · 56 display-1 (32 on phones). Zilla 600, −0.01em, at 40px and above. Mono capitals are for labels and actions only. All-caps Icelandic takes a line-height of at least 1.15.

Casing: the product name is capitalised wherever it is written as a word: **Wottle**, **Orðusta** (the ledger wordmark, the slip wordmark, tab titles, metadata, prose). The locale registry (`lib/i18n/locales.ts`, `wordmark`) is its one source and the copy reads it. Identifiers, URLs, cookie names and file names stay lowercase. Sentence case for sentences; mono labels uppercase. No italics anywhere in the room.

## 4. Space and layout

- 4px base. The room gutter is 56px (≥1100px) or 40px (900–1100px); the ledger is 340px (≥1100px) or 260px (900–1100px). Bars (lobby, queue) are 60px (56px on phones), 12px from the field.
- **Match layout: one grid** (spec 068, 23 September 2026). In the match states (table, starting, live, match over, void) the left column is the stack `scoreboard / field`, starting at y=24 with a 12px gap between the box and the field; there are no bars. The right column is the ledger, laid on the same grid so every horizontal edge on the right is level with one on the left: its head's three rows mirror the scoreboard's three, and the face-off header's 1.5px `--ink` rule is level with the box's bottom border; after the same 12px gap (and the field's 1.5px frame) ten move rows, each exactly one cell tall, sit level with the board's rows.
- **Field size, match**: cells are a whole number of pixels: `cell = min(floor((available − 3) / 10), 71)`, where available is the smaller of the height left after the scoreboard, the gap and the margins, and the width left beside the ledger; the field is `10 × cell + 3` (ten cells inside a 1.5px frame). At 1440×900 the field is 713px (ten 71px cells). Bounding the width as well as the height keeps the room from overflowing between 901 and 1100px wide. The ledger reads the same cell (`--cell-size`). Never below the fold; never scrolls; computed with a ResizeObserver, not viewport units.
- **Field size, lobby and queue**: the largest square that fits after the two bars are placed, `min(availableHeight − 2×60 − 2×12 − 48, 720)`, also bounded by the width beside the ledger. The ledger is the height of the stack `bar / field / bar`, never of the viewport, and is never stretched.
- **Phone match** (below 900px): a single column. The scoreboard at the field's width, the field, then the ledger block: the live row (two lines; a button, `aria-expanded`, whose right cell reads `history ▸`), territory, and a foot pinned to the bottom edge with the safe area, holding `⋯` (44×44) and the match's language (`english words` / `íslensk orð`). No wordmark and no clock in the phone ledger during a match. Below 700px tall the territory hides first. Checked at 390×844, 390×664 and 360×640. Lobby and queue on a phone keep `bar / field / bar / live row`.
- The sheet the live row opens sits **in flow between the field and the foot** — it takes the space left in the ledger column and scrolls inside it. It is never fixed, never has a backdrop, and never covers the scoreboard, the field or the foot: nothing but the slip is placed over the field, on a phone least of all. Escape or `close` returns focus to the live row.
- At 390×844 the page itself does not scroll, with the sheet closed or open. The field fits the full width less the room's 16px gutters, so cells are 35px with the whole cell as hit target. Discrete controls are ≥44px; the 35px cell is the grid's own floor and meets WCAG 2.5.8 (AA).
- Flush-left alignment everywhere; numerals on the scoreboard sit in its right-hand value column.

## 5. Components

### 5.1 Field

A ruled grid of one hundred capitals. 1.5px `--ink` frame; 1px `--rule` between cells; flat `--paper` cells. **Turn frame** (spec 048, amended by spec 050): while a move is the viewer's to make, a 3px outline in `--you` is drawn inside the frame (`outline-offset: -3px`, so the geometry never moves); it returns to the ink rule from commit until their own reveal has held, and for good once they have ten moves; it is ink, and the field takes no pick, while the viewer is offline (§5.3). The opponent's moves never change it. Signed out, the field is the frame and the rules with no letters (§5.9 sign-in); so it is at the table and after a void, since the server sends no letters until both players have sat down (spec 069). Letter centred in `--font-board` 600. Value numeral in the top-right gutter (`top:4%; right:6%`) in `--font-mono` 400, `--muted`; on a scored letter it takes the scorer's seat colour (`--opp-text` for the opponent, as it is text under 17px); on a picked letter it is `--ink` 500. The scorer of a letter is the player who froze it first (spec 049).

Letter states (each has exactly one mark):
| State | Mark |
| --- | --- |
| free | `--ink` letter, muted numeral |
| picked (yours) | your seat colour, `scale(1.08)`, inset 2px `--ink` ring, numeral `--ink` |
| scored / frozen | the owner's seat colour, letter and numeral, inside the owner's band; a letter keeps the colour of the player who froze it first, whatever crosses it later |
| illegal pick | 300ms shake in its own colour; live row states the fact |
| refused move | the two letters return to their places (150ms exchange); live row states the reason for two seconds |
| opponent's exchange | the two letters travel to each other's places (150ms) on your field when their move resolves |
| last moved | a 2px bar in the mover's seat colour along the bottom inner edge of each of the two cells of that player's most recent **resolved** swap; at most two cells per seat; dropped when the letter freezes (the band then marks it). A refused move never becomes the last move, so after a reload onto a refused move there is no tick until that player moves again. The cell's accessible name ends `, Kári's last move` (spec 068) |
| keyboard focus | 2px `--ink` outline at −4px offset |

### 5.2 Word band

One band per scored word record. 14% tint of the scorer's seat colour; square ends aligned to the cell grid; inset 20% of a cell across its short axis (leaves the numeral gutter clean) and 5% along its long axis (never enters the neighbouring cell). A 1.5px chevron in the seat colour, opened to about 150° (arm depth 9% of a cell across the band's height), sits at the end where reading **begins**: left edge pointing right (ltr), right edge pointing left (rtl), top pointing down (ttb), bottom pointing up (btt). A run valid in both directions scores **once**, read forward, so every band carries exactly one chevron (rules §3.1, decided 14 September 2026). Bands of the same seat never touch end to end (the whole-run rule guarantees it). A band shades its whole word, crossings included; the crossing letter keeps the colour of the player who froze it first, so it sits in its own ink over both tints (spec 049, amended 21 September 2026). The chevron sits at the word's reading start, over the other seat's letter when that is where reading begins.

### 5.3 Scoreboard (match) and player bar (lobby, queue)

**Scoreboard** (spec 068, 23 September 2026). In the match states (table, starting, live, match over, void; the table and the void added by spec 069) one box sits above the field and holds the match's clock and both players; there are no player bars around the field. A 1.5px `--ink` frame on `--paper`, three rows sharing one grid: desktop rows 40px, columns `216px · track · 64px`, 16px gaps, 14px padding; phone rows 34px, columns `112 · track · 36`, 10px gaps, 8px padding, the box as wide as the field.

1. **The clock.** Left, a two-line label: the phase in words (`match clock`, `under a minute`, `last 12s`, `time`, `starts in 3`, `match over`) over the detail — the pace `≈27s a move` while a move is yours, `4:52 of 5:00` at match over. Middle, the track: ten 30s blocks of six 5s ticks; one tick empties every 5s from the right (`ticks = ceil(seconds / 5)`). On a phone the blocks fill in 5s steps without tick marks. Right, the numeral in mono 24px, tabular.
2. **The opponent.** A 10px square in the seat colour, the name (Zilla 600 15px) and a mono 10px uppercase `--muted` sub-line (`1265 · 6 of 10 · playing`); their ten moves on the track; their total (mono 26px in the seat colour; on a phone 20px in `--opp-text`, since 20px is under the large-text size). The row names no seat word: its square and place say who it is.
3. **You**, nearest the board you play on. The same parts in your seat colour; only your row names its seat (`1310 · you · move 4 of 10`).

**One track column** for all three rows: each clock block stands directly above the matching move of each player, so time left and moves left compare straight down a column. A player's ten moves empty from the right as they resolve; a move in flight shows at 30% of the seat colour until it resolves (a refusal fills it back); the moves left are outlined (1.5px inset) rather than filled when that player is reconnecting or gone, or, on your own row, when you are offline.

**Sub-lines.** Yours: `move 4 of 10` (in `--you` while the move is yours), `move 4 of 10 · scoring`, `10 of 10 · done`, and `move 8 · behind pace` in your seat colour when moves left − seconds left ÷ 30 ≥ 1 (colour is never the only carrier, so the words say it); your own connection, `offline · reconnecting`. Theirs: `6 of 10 · playing`, `6 of 10 · scoring`, `10 of 10 · done`; `reconnecting · 0:42 left` during the 90s window and `8 of 10 · gone for 2:04` after it (never a frozen `0:00 left`), measured on the server-corrected clock. Phone forms are short: `6 of 10`, `move 4`, `gone for 2:04`, `behind pace`, `offline`.

**The table** (spec 069). Before anyone plays: the clock row reads `match clock` over `starts when both sit`, with a full track that does not run and `5:00`; the opponent's row `1187 · opponent · on the way`, then `· ready` when they sit; yours `1204 · you · not ready` or `· ready`. Neither row shows a total. On a phone the rows carry the status alone (`on the way`, `ready`). **The void:** the clock row's detail reads `not started`; the opponent's row `did not sit down` or `left`; yours `you · searching` when the void put you back in the queue.

**Starting** (the 3·2·1, which follows the table and is anchored to the server's start, the moment the second player sat + 4.5s; there is no grace period and no queue countdown of its own): both rows read `ready`, the label reads `starts in 3`, and the clock row's track loads left to right over the count. **Match over:** the clock holds the time that was left (`match over` over `4:52 of 5:00`), and the player rows carry the rating lines (`1191 → 1203 · +12`), or `rating pending` until they arrive; an opponent who has left the result adds `· has left` (spec 071). **A rematch** names its series under the clock's label whenever no pace is shown: `match 2 · Birna 1–0` (spec 071; the series is a fact about the match). **Review** (spec 071): the clock row is the **scrubber**: the label `step 7 of 20` over `the clock at step 7` (phone `step 7` over `the clock then`), a 10px bar at `step ÷ steps` (4px on a phone) in place of the track, and the numeral as the clock read when that move was received (`3:31`). The player rows show each total and moves played at the step (`1204 · 3 of 10 at step 7`; phone `3 of 10`); no pace, no urgency.

**Urgency is weight only; nothing blinks.** Running: `--paper` ground, `--muted` ticks, numeral 500. Under 1:00 and in the last 15 seconds: `--tint` ground, `--ink` ticks, numeral 700; in the last 15 seconds the label reads `last 12s` in `--ink` 600. At 0:00 the track is empty and the label reads `time`.

**Player bar** (retired from the lobby and the queue by spec 070: the lobby is a page with your block, §5.10, and the search lives in the line slot; the paragraph below describes the bar as it stood and is kept only for the fixtures that still render it). 60px, `1fr auto`. Left: 12px square in the seat colour (1.5px dashed outline when the seat is empty) + name + one-line mono sub-line (`1204 · you`, `1191 · opponent`, `searching · 0:07 · cancel ▸`). The queue's empty seat also says where the search stands (spec 069): `search paused · resume ▸` when a hidden tab paused it; at 3:00 `Still searching? · 3:00` with the primary `keep searching ▸` and a 30s drain; unanswered, `search stopped · find again ▸`. During the table-leave cooldown the lobby's find action reads `find again in 4:12`, counting down, and is not a control. Right: the primary action when the seat is empty or searching; a signed-in seat outside a match shows no total. The bar's edge nearest the field is a bare `--rule` track; searching, the `lane-search` sweep on a single 4px track. The opponent's bar is always on top, yours always at the bottom.

### 5.4 Ledger

1.5px `--ink` top rule; height = the stack's height. There is no clock in the ledger: the match clock is the scoreboard's first row (§5.3; the ledger clock and its last-15-seconds face were removed 23 September 2026, spec 068). <!-- retired-name -->

**The head** (match states) has three rows that mirror the scoreboard's three: row 1 the caption (the wordmark `Wottle` / `Orðusta` left; right, the mono context — nothing during a live match, `final · 4:52` once the match is over — and the `⋯` menu, which sits in the caption during a match and opens downward; there is no desktop foot during a match); row 2 the territory bar with its counts (`10 · 80 free · 10`) or, in the final state, the verdict; row 3 the face-off header, whose 1.5px `--ink` rule is level with the scoreboard box's bottom border. A notice (`realtime lost · polling`, a rematch line) takes the **second line of row 2**, the state row: beside the territory bar during a match, under the verdict's score line in the final state, so the ledger still ends level with the field. In the final state on desktop the totals row is dropped (the scoreboard rows carry the totals), and the final actions (`result ▸`, `lobby`) sit in the caption beside `⋯`, whose final menu gains `how to play`; on a phone the sheet keeps the totals row and the foot. In the lobby and the queue the caption carries their contexts and the foot holds the actions. **At the table** (spec 069) the caption context reads `opponent found` (after a void, `no match`), the territory line `0 · 100 free · 0`, and the ten move rows are empty with no live row. During the 3·2·1 the live row reads `move 1` over `pick when the clock starts`; the count itself is the scoreboard's. The column header is a face-off across the **spine**, 21 September 2026: `Birna · you ■` right-aligned over your column, mono `move` over the spine, `■ Kári` left-aligned over theirs; its rule is `--ink`, the one ink rule inside the ledger. Then, after the same 12px gap as the scoreboard's (and the field's 1.5px frame), the moves table: ten grid rows **indexed by move number**, `1fr 40px 1fr` inside each: your column, the **spine**, theirs. The spine is a 40px column ruled `--rule` on both sides and carries the move number in mono 11px, `--muted` once either seat has played the move, `#B9B4A6` before; it separates the players on every row. Row N holds your Nth move in your column and theirs in theirs, so the two columns fill at their own pace and a row can be your future and their past at once; the rule between rows is `--rule` and belongs to the row, so it is one continuous line; each row is exactly one cell tall (`--cell-size`), level with a row of the board. Each cell reads **inward to the spine**: your words right-aligned with the move's points beside the spine (`BORÐ 22`), theirs left-aligned mirrored (`15 GILT`); points in mono 13px 600 `--ink`; words in seat colour joined by `·`, wrapping. A **miss** writes its penalty beside the spine with a mono 10px uppercase `--muted` label on the outer side: `no word −5` when a resolved move scored nothing, `not played −5` when the clock ran out first (rules §5.6). Only the number is `--err` crimson (through `PointsLost`); a floored 0 is `--muted`. A refused move writes nothing. Then the **totals row** in the final state only (both totals 24px in seat colour, facing across the spine with mono `total` in it, closed by a 1.5px `--ink` rule) → hint line (match-level lines only, hidden when empty) → notices → foot outside a live match (`how to play ▸` left in the lobby and final; `result ▸` in the final once the slip has been lifted; the state's actions right). The **live row** is the row of the viewer's next open move: the grid row itself with `--tint` background and a 3px `--ink` left rule, the two lines as one band across the ledger (the beat names the move, so the spine breaks here); their Nth move's points, if they have played it, sit top-right on line 1. Line 1 is the move's beat in `--font-board` 600 17px (`move 4 · your move`, `move 4 · scoring`, `move 4 scored`, `10 of 10 played`, `time · scoring`); line 2, in `--muted` mono, is the field's instruction while a move is yours to make (`pick a letter`, `picking · T (2) · tap a second letter`) or the beat's fact otherwise (`you +13 · move 5 opens`, `Kári · 8 of 10 · 1:12 left`); when the fact is empty the row collapses to one line. After your reveal the scored row **holds** as the tinted row for 600ms (the **move hold**) before the next live row opens; the field takes no pick meanwhile. The opponent's reveal writes into their column as it lands and holds nothing. Notices (transport lines, a rematch line) are rendered as live-row-styled lines; a pick cleared, a refusal, a submit error and your own connection are never notices: they are line 2. Challenges are not notices (spec 070): a call, a challenge you sent and what became of it are the line slot's (§5.10), on every page; the resign confirmation, the end-early offer and the rematch request are on the slip (§5.9); once the end-early slip is put away, its offer is line 2. Hovering a row lights its bands on the field. **On the result** (spec 071) an incoming rematch, once the slip is lifted, is a two-line notice in the state row (`Kári asks for a rematch · 0:24` over `accept ▸ · decline`, a 4px drain along its bottom); with a third party's call too, both share the row one line each, the rematch first. The ledger's verdict keeps the detail's first two clauses. **In review** the caption reads `review · 4:52`, the state row holds the step controls (`first · back · play ▸ · next · last`), every move cell is a way to its step (muted and named `not yet reached` after the current step), and the step's own row carries the **cursor line** in the live row's style: line 1 `move 3 · Birna · LEK · ÆSKU +33`, line 2 `froze 6 · Birna leads 51–18` (a refusal `refused · frozen`; the closing step `time · −15 not played` in the first unplayed row). On a phone the cursor line takes the live row and the pinned foot holds `◂ result` and five 44px glyphs `◂◂ ◂ ▶ ▸ ▸▸`. The ledger never scrolls; if a row would exceed three lines, moves older than the last three collapse to totals.

**Line 2 shows one thing** (spec 068, Phase B). When several apply, line 2 shows exactly one, in this order: offline > back > submit error > refused or illegal pick > pick cleared > end-early offer > missed beat or stakes > the instruction or fact. Each source holds on its own timer: a refusal, an illegal pick, a pick cleared and a submit error 2s; back 4s. A held line is replaced at once by a higher one and resumes only if its time is left. The sources:

- **offline**: `offline · reconnecting`, while your own connection is down (§5.3).
- **back**: `back · away 0:34 · the clock ran on`, for 4s after your connection returns.
- **submit error**: the failure's own line (the `errors` copy), for 2s.
- **refused move**: `frozen · Kári froze it · pick another` / `moved · Kári moved it · pick another`, for 2s. **Illegal pick** names the word it would break: `frozen · GILT · Kári · pick another`, for 2s.
- **pick cleared**: `pick cleared · Kári moved that letter`, for 2s, when their move touches your pick.
- **end-early offer**: `Kári is gone · end the match ▸`, a secondary action, once you have put the end-early slip away (§5.9).
- **missed beat**: when your resolved move scored no word, line 1 reads `move 4 · no word` and line 2 `−5 · move 5 opens`, or the floored loss `−3 · a total never falls below 0`. **Stakes**: under 1:00, with a move yours and nothing picked, `3 moves left · −15 if unplayed` (`1 move left` for one), priced by the rules' timeout penalty; `3 moves left · nothing to lose` when nothing can be lost. The number is crimson through `PointsLost`; `if unplayed` stays `--muted`.

**Line 2 never wraps** at 1440×900. It runs under the opponent's points, which sit on line 1 only, so it has the ledger's width: about 40 mono characters, with an ellipsis for a long name. A test renders every line 2 string in both languages; a string that does not fit is shortened in the copy, never by widening the ledger (§8 lists the shortened strings).

**Phone ledger, during a match.** Beneath the field: the live row (two lines, `history ▸`), the territory bar, and the foot pinned to the bottom edge with the safe area, holding `⋯` (44×44) and the match's language (`english words` / `íslensk orð`). There is no wordmark and no clock in the phone ledger during a match; the scoreboard above the field carries the clock. Below 700px tall the territory hides first. The sheet opens between the field and the foot, never over the scoreboard or the foot.

### 5.5 Primary action

Text in `--font-mono` 12px uppercase 0.12em on an `--ink` fill with `--paper` text, `10px 14px` padding, square, followed by ` ▸`. One per screen at most (`enter the lobby ▸`, `find an opponent ▸`). On a page the primary is the page's (spec 070): the door's `enter the lobby ▸`, the lobby's `find an opponent ▸`. When the line slot carries a call, its `accept ▸` is the page's one primary and the page's own drops to a secondary; an open composer's `send challenge ▸` does the same, unless a call is up, when send is drawn as a secondary. On a phone the page primary is pinned above the bottom slot and hidden whenever another primary holds the screen. Secondary actions are mono uppercase text with ` ▸` in `--ink` (`challenge ▸`, `rematch ▸`, `cancel ▸`). Destructive actions have no colour of their own; they are confirmed on a slip (§5.9), where the safe action is the primary and holds focus (`keep playing ▸` over `yes, resign ▸`).

**Interaction states** (21 September 2026: players could not tell what was clickable). Every action has a hover, a pressed and a keyboard-focus state, all within the nine tokens and the §6 120ms ease. **Primary:** on hover it inverts to `--paper` with `--ink` text inside a 1.5px `--ink` inset frame (the slip's frame); pressed, the ground is `--tint`. **Secondary:** on hover a 1px underline, 4px below the text; pressed, `--muted`. **Menu items** tint their row with `--tint` instead of underlining; the open `⋯` trigger keeps the tint. **Keyboard focus:** a 2px `--ink` outline 3px outside the action; a pointer click leaves no ring. **Disabled** actions never react. **The field:** a free letter takes `--tint` under the pointer, only while a move is the viewer's to make. **Names** that link to a profile keep their ink and underline on hover or focus. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the states change without the ease.

**Guard and focus** (spec 070 FR-015, FR-016). A control that appears or changes meaning under the pointer (the line slot's actions, the composer's send, every slip action) ignores activation for 500ms. Nothing that arrives takes focus: a call in the line slot is announced politely, and while it is up a skip link (`answer the challenge from Kári` / `svara áskoruninni · Kári`) is the page's first focusable element. A composer the viewer opened takes focus on its send; Esc or `not now` returns it to the row's `challenge ▸`. A slip the viewer raised focuses its safe action (`keep playing ▸`, `stay ▸`).

### 5.6 Tables (directory, matches, best words)

Same grammar as the ledger: ruled rows (1px `--rule`), first column in `--font-board` 600, numbers in `--font-mono` 12px, actions as mono uppercase text with ` ▸`. No cards, no avatars, no skeletons; while loading, rows show `—`.

### 5.7 Inputs

One input per page (spec 070): the door's name field (the profile rename is phase 2). It is never autofocused. Text in `--font-board` 600 17px on a 1.5px `--ink` underline; placeholder `--muted`. No boxes, no radii.

### 5.8 Chart (profile)

A `<svg>` polyline, 1.5px in the seat colour, over three 1px `--rule` gridlines, framed by 1px `--ink` rules on the left and bottom, mono axis labels. No area fill, no markers, no tooltip. On a profile (spec 072) it spans the last 30 days, 708×200 on desktop and 358×140 on a phone, labelled `30 days ago` and `today`; with no match in the window it is a flat line at the rating, labelled `1212 · no matches in the last 30 days`.

### 5.9 Slip

A slip the game raises on its own (ready, void, end early, match over) focuses its headline (`tabindex=-1`, no focus ring), not an action, so a stray key press cannot act on it; the ready and void slips are derived from the match state, never raised by a press. **The table** (spec 069). Every match begins at the table: the field is the empty ruled frame at 32% under the ready slip, and the server holds the letters until both players have sat down. A player whose own press made the match, or whose tab was visible and in use in the last 30s, is seated at once; anyone else has 20s and `ready ▸`. When the second player sits, the server sets the start 4.5s ahead; both seat lines read `ready`, the label `starts in 3`, and the slip lifts 3.3s before go, when the scoreboard's 3·2·1 (§5.3) takes over. If the table does not fill in 20s, or a player leaves it before go, the slip rewrites in place, without motion, as the void slip: nothing is rated or recorded.

`keep waiting ▸` puts the end-early slip away for the rest of the match: the game never raises it again (the old 10-second re-raise is gone), and the offer moves to the live row's line 2 as a secondary action (`Kári is gone · end the match ▸`, §5.4).

The match-over slip lands 600ms after the final reveal has settled and held (at once on a reload with nothing to reveal); `review the match ▸` lifts it and `result ▸` in the ledger foot brings it back.

On a phone the detail keeps its first two clauses. A counted line beside a default result would be a lie. The label line counts the match and nothing more (amended 20 September 2026, after a live match read `match over · 1 rounds · 0:00 · resigned` for a player who had merely disconnected). Signed out there is no room: `/` and `/en` are the door, and a room URL sends the visitor there with a `?next=` that is spent after they enter (spec 070).

## 6. Motion

Motion is a state change, not a performance. Durations: ring changes 120ms; letter exchange 150ms (on commit — the two letters travel to each other's places, they never teleport); band draw 400ms per word, staggered 120ms; total count-up 400ms; found-opponent name write 200ms; the scoreboard's clock track loading left to right over the 3·2·1; the ready slip lifting 3.3s before go (150ms fade) while the letters land row by row during `3` (about 720ms); setting-field letters ~100ms apart. Easing `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.2,1)`. Geometry does not animate except the two listed (commit exchange, picked scale); the turn frame is an outline. The slip (§5.9) fades in and out with the field over 150ms and is the only thing ever placed over the field. The move hold is 600ms (spec 050; it was 1200ms as the settle hold) and is a reading pause, not motion: it stays under `prefers-reduced-motion`, where everything else is 0ms and end-state only.

**Time is not motion.** The scoreboard's numeral and ticks step once a second, and the 3·2·1 counts, the ready slip's 20s count and its drain bar step once a second (spec 069), under `prefers-reduced-motion` as everywhere else. **Nothing blinks**: urgency is weight and ground (§5.3), never a flash (the ledger clock's once-a-second flash and its `clock-flash` keyframe were removed 23 September 2026, spec 068). <!-- retired-name -->

Sounds: `tile-select` on pick, `valid-swap` on commit (with haptic where available), band-draw tick on reveal; `challenge` (two notes) when a table opens in a hidden tab, and `match-start` at go (spec 069). Nothing on cancel or error. Sound is toggled from the `⋯` menu.

## 7. The five beats

Each move has five beats (amended 21 September 2026, spec 050). They are the viewer's; the opponent's moves land on the viewer's field as a sixth, non-blocking beat.

| Beat | Field | Scoreboard | Ledger |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Set | untouched; the turn frame in `--you` | your row at your count; `· move 4 of 10` in `--you` | your next row opens: `move 4 · your move` over `pick a letter`; the rightmost filled move on your row is the move you are making |
| Think | pick marks; the opponent's exchanges land around them | — | `move 4 · your move` over `picking · T (2) · tap a second letter` |
| Commit | your two letters exchange in place; the frame returns to ink; no pick | `· move 4 of 10 · scoring` in `--muted`; the move in flight at 30% | `move 4 · scoring`, one line |
| Reveal | bands draw along each word (30% tint); no pick | your total counts up | words and points written into the row |
| Settle | tint settles to 14%; the last-moved tick marks your two cells; no pick for 600ms | the rightmost move on your row empties | the scored row holds: `move 4 scored` over `you +13 · move 5 opens`; then territory updates and the next row opens |
| Settle, no word | no band; the last-moved tick marks your two cells; no pick for 600ms | the rightmost move on your row empties; your total drops | the missed beat holds: `move 4 · no word` over `−5 · move 5 opens` (or `−3 · a total never falls below 0`), the number crimson |
| Their move (any time) | their two letters exchange; their bands draw at 30% and settle; their last-moved tick moves to the two cells; a pick of yours on a touched letter clears | their total counts up; `· 6 of 10 · scoring` → `· 7 of 10 · playing`; the rightmost move on their row empties | their words and points written into their column of row 7; your line 2 reads `pick cleared · Kári moved that letter` for 2s if it cleared your pick, and is otherwise untouched; the move is announced once, politely (§9) |

A refused move (`frozen`, `moved`) skips Reveal and Settle: the letters return, the live row states the reason for two seconds, the frame is back in `--you`, the count is unchanged, and the last-moved tick stays where it was: a refused move is never the last move.

Under 1:00, while a move is yours and nothing is picked, line 2 states the stakes instead of `pick a letter`: `3 moves left · −15 if unplayed`. While you are offline the frame is ink and the field takes no pick, whatever the beat.

## 8. Copy

- Sentence case for sentences; mono uppercase for labels; the product name capitalised, `Wottle` / `Orðusta` (§3).
- One idea per line. State the fact, then the next action: `frozen · GILT · Kári · pick another`; `No runs yet. Start one from the lobby.`
- No exclamation marks. No apologies. No metaphors about speed, power or brains. Never personify the system.

## 9. Accessibility

- Every cell: `role="gridcell"`, `aria-label="row 8, column F, T, value 2, free"` (state word last: free / picked / frozen by Kári). A cell in a player's last move ends `, Kári's last move` (Icelandic `, síðasti leikur · Kári`). Coordinates live only here; they are never printed.
- Keyboard: arrows move focus on the field; Space or Enter picks, and on a second letter commits the move; Esc cancels a pick (and is the slip's cancel); `M` toggles sound. The `?` hotkey retired with the in-room rules (spec 048).
- Slip: `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, labelled by its headline; focus moves to its primary action (on the resign slip, the safe `keep playing ▸`) and returns to the opener when it lifts; a slip the game raises on its own (ready, void, end early) focuses its headline instead (`tabindex=-1`, no focus ring; `useFocusTrap` honours a non-tabbable initial focus), and its primary ignores activation for 500ms after it appears (the ready slip's `ready ▸` is never focused); at the table each seat change (`Kári · ready`) is announced politely; the headline is inside a `role="status" aria-live="assertive"` region so it is announced once.
- Scoreboard: a labelled `section`. The clock row is a `role="timer"` named from its label, detail and numeral (`match clock, ≈27s a move, 3:12 left`), updated once a second and not live (`aria-live="off"`; the live row announces the beats); its tick graphics are `aria-hidden`. Each player's track is a `role="progressbar"`, `aria-valuemin=0 aria-valuemax=10 aria-valuenow=<moves left>`, `aria-valuetext="7 of 10 moves left"`. The seat squares are `aria-hidden`.
- Result and review (spec 071): the match-over slip focuses its headline and its actions ignore activation for 500ms; an incoming rematch is announced politely (the slip's line, or the ledger's). The review scrubber is one `role="slider"` (`aria-valuemin=1`, `aria-valuemax=<steps>`, `aria-valuenow=<step>`, `aria-valuetext="step 7 of 20, Birna, LEK ÆSKU plus 33"`) that takes focus and owns ←/→, Home/End and Space (play) while it has it; the field's arrows are untouched. The ledger in review is one `role="grid"` with a roving tabindex: arrows and Home/End move among its cells, Enter or Space jumps to the cell's step. Review's controls carry words on desktop and `aria-label`s on the phone's glyphs; autoplay stops on any input and when the tab hides.
- Focus at go: when the 3·2·1 ends (the count from the server's start, spec 069), focus moves to the field (its first cell) and the live row announces line 1.
- Opponent announcements: a polite, visually hidden region (`room-announcer`) says each opponent move once as it arrives live, after the viewer's own beat: `Kári SKÓ +11 · 5 of 10`, or `Kári no word −5 · 6 of 10` (`Kári SKÓ +11 · 5 af 10`, `Kári ekkert orð −5 · 6 af 10`). It is keyed by the move's receipt sequence, so a poll or a reload never replays one, and says at most one line per 1.5s, the newest waiting line winning. It also says `1:00 left` and `0:15 left` (`… eftir`) once each as the clock crosses them, never on a reload past them.
- Live row: `aria-live="polite"` (line 1 announces each beat once); verdict: `aria-live="assertive"` once. The scoreboard's tracks are the progress marks for moves (see §5.3); the ledger names no move of the viewer's.
- Colour is never the only carrier: seat is also position (your row nearest the board, your column left of the spine), the square before the name, and the `you` word in your sub-line; band direction is the chevron; clock state is the label's words (`under a minute`, `last 12s`) and the numeral's weight; being behind is the words `behind pace`; a lost point is the label beside its crimson number; each player's progress is their track's length and the count in their sub-line.
- Contrast floors: text ≥ 4.5:1 (`--ink`, `--muted`, `--you`, `--opp-text`, `--err`); `--opp` terracotta text only ≥ 17px.
- Reduced motion honoured everywhere (§6).

## 10. Do not

- Do not add a colour, radius, shadow, gradient, blur or font outside §2–§3.
- Do not place anything over the field but the slip (§5.9): no banners, toasts, other overlays or confetti, and no slip for anything but its six kinds. On a page nothing is placed over the content either: a standing state is the line slot's.
- Do not add a card. If information needs a container, it is a ruled row in the ledger or a row of the scoreboard.
- Do not show a number outside the scoreboard, the bars and the ledger (letter values on the field are the one exception).
- Do not draw the clock anywhere but the scoreboard's first row, and do not put player bars around the field in a match.
- Do not add an avatar, an icon set, an illustration or an emoji. The ink square and the chevron are the only marks.
- Do not bind colour to player slots; bind it to seats.
- Do not colour anything crimson but a number of points lost. Not its label, not a seat, not a rating change, not urgency, not a frame, focus or control.
- Do not make anything blink. Urgency is weight and ground; time steps once a second.
- Do not put a field on a page or a masthead in the room. A new screen is either a room state (it has the field) or a page (§5.10: masthead, line slot, content, folio). The rules page teaches; it never plays.
