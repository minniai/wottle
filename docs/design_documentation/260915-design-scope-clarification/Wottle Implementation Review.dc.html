Built, but never seen

The Field & Ledger rebuild (spec 044) is structurally complete: one room, seat colours, bands with chevrons, the ledger, every state. Three paint defects in the field, an unwired phone ledger and a handful of deviations stand between it and the design.

01
Verdict

The architecture is right; the pixels were never checked

Every object the design asked for exists and is where it should be: Room measures the field with a ResizeObserver, PlayerBar carries the lane on its inner edge, FieldBands draws one tinted band and one chevron per word record from the reading direction, Ledger shares ten rows across its height and folds them, notices are live-row lines, colour is resolved through one seat function, the seven tokens and two fonts are the only ones declared, and the old components are gone from the tree. The reveal choreography, the disconnect line, the verdict block, the warm-up field, the placeholder queue and the profile on the room grid are all there.

What is not there is a rendered check. The task log records the Playwright suite and the reference screenshots as not run locally — no Supabase, and the unit tests run in JSDOM, which does not paint. As a result three defects shipped that any screenshot would have caught: the field's paper ground is hidden under its own rule colour (finding A1), the letter size never follows the cell size (A2), and the chevron is a 0.15px hairline (A3). Section 02 reproduces the field with the stylesheet as written.

Five of the design's open questions were decided by the team on 14 September (section 04). Three of those decisions change the design's copy and scale, and the implementation follows the decisions, not the documents. That is correct behaviour, and the design bundle should now be updated to match.

03
Findings

Twenty-five findings in five groups

A: visible defects that make the room look unlike the design. B: implemented, but not as specified. C: specified, not implemented. D: correct, but left over from the previous look. E: process. File and line references are in the companion markdown.

A · Visible defects
A1
The field ground is grey, not paper, and the cell rules are invisible. .field has background:var(--rule), cells are made transparent, and the paper ::before at z-index −1 is painted behind the field because the field creates no stacking context. Isolating the field is not the fix either: the pseudo would then cover the gaps and the rules would vanish into paper.
Paint .field paper, drop the gap and the pseudo, draw the rules as border-right/-bottom: 1px solid var(--rule) on each cell (none on the last column and row), as Fig. 2 does. Bands stay under the cells. Browser-mode test: field background #FFFDF7, cell border #E6E2D6.
A2
Letters do not scale with the cell. --cell-size is read with a 48px fallback and never declared: letters are always 26.4px (37% of a 72px desktop cell; 73% of a 36px phone cell) and numerals 8.6px.
Declare --cell-size: calc(var(--field-size) / 10) on .field. Below 900px derive it from the measured width.
A3
Chevrons are hairlines. strokeWidth={0.15} together with vectorEffect="non-scaling-stroke" means 0.15 device pixels. The design is 1.5px.
Set strokeWidth={1.5}; keep non-scaling-stroke.
A4
On phones the whole ledger stacks under the bottom bar and the page scrolls. Below 900px the grid becomes one column and the ledger renders in full; LedgerSheet is imported only by its own test. The phone Playwright check passes because the live row happens to be near the top of the stacked ledger.
Below 900px render caption + live row + territory + history ▸; open LedgerSheet from it. Assert scrollHeight ≤ innerHeight at 390×844.
B · Implemented, not as specified
B1
The stack is centred in its column. .room__stack{margin:0 auto} puts slack on both sides of the field; at 1440×900 the field-to-ledger gutter is about 168px, not 56. Design system §4: flush-left, one 56px gutter.
Either margin:0 on the stack, or size the first column auto and centre the whole room with justify-content:center. Recommended: the second.
B2
Live row tint stops short of the round label. The tinted cell spans columns 2–3 (gridColumn:"span 2"); the label sits on paper outside it. Fig. 2 tints the whole row with the 3px rule at its left edge.
Render the live row as one element spanning all three columns with the label inside.
B3
picking · T (0) — the letter value is hard-coded to 0 in the controller's live state.
Look the value up from LETTER_SCORING_VALUES_IS.
B4
frozen · Kári R<n> names the current round, not the round the word froze in; FrozenTile carries no round.
Find the accumulated word covering the cell and use its roundNumber.
B5
Motion: the preview does not exchange in 150ms — letters swap instantly; the 200ms pin fade is declared as a keyframe but never applied; the found-opponent name has no 200ms write.
FLIP the two letter spans with a 150ms transform; apply pin-fade on settle; fade the name in.
B6
Disconnected lane uses a CSS dashed border, whose dash pattern the browser chooses (about 12/12), not the 6px/4px pattern.
An inline <svg> line with stroke-dasharray="6 4"; gradients are banned.
B7
Queue ledger has no live row. setting the field · 58 of 100 letters and round 1 in 3 print in the plain hint line, not a tinted live row as in Fig. 7.
Give the queue variant one live-row element above the hint.
B8
Bars are inset 4px (padding:0 4px); the seat square and the total no longer align with the field's frame.
Padding 0.
B9
Profile chart labels stretch. preserveAspectRatio="none" on a 600×180 viewBox distorts the mono axis text at any other aspect.
Measure the container and set the viewBox width to it, or render labels as HTML beside the SVG.
B10
A bare page outside the room. /match/[id] returns an unstyled <div>Match not found</div>.
Redirect to the lobby with a notice line: that match does not exist.
B11
Lobby copy says challenge for a ranked match; the plan says unranked, and the invite path has no ranked flag, so challenge matches are rated exactly like queue matches.
Decided 15 September 2026: unranked (section 06, item 1). The invite path gets a rated flag; captions and lobby copy say unranked.

04
Team decisions
14 Sep 2026

Five questions answered; the implementation follows the answers

Recorded in specs/044-field-ledger-redesign/spec.md (Decisions) and docs/prd_and_requirements/wottle_game_rules.md §2a, §3.1. None is a defect; each changes what the design bundle should say.

  1. Clock budget stays 5:00. Lane full width = 5:00, aria-valuemax=300, copy ranked · 10 rounds · 5:00 clocks, first-match line says five minutes. Implemented consistently.
  2. Second tap commits; preview is opt-in from the ⋯ menu, persisted with the sound preference. The preview is priced by a read-only server action (previewSwap), not client-side: the 55MB dictionary cannot ship to the browser. The hint reads tap again to play until the price lands. Sound.
  3. Queue field is a placeholder seeded per player; letters that differ swap in when the real board arrives. The live-row clause an opponent joins when the last one lands is dropped. Implemented.
  4. One record per run. A run valid both ways (FÁR/RÁF) scores once, read forward, so every band carries one chevron. Pinned by doubleReading.test.ts. Design system §5.2's two-chevron clause is moot.
  5. 0:00 means a timeout pass in every remaining round; the player keeps score and territory; both at 0:00 ends the match. Renders as 0:00 with an empty lane. Implemented server-side; the bar renders it.

06
Decisions
given

Three decisions, given 15 September 2026

The team took the review's recommendation on all three. They are binding for the completion (handoff R6, tasks T030–T032), not defaults.

  1. Ranked challenges → unranked. The code rates every match and the lobby copy says so; the plan recommended unranked challenges. Decided: unranked, because a directory challenge lets a player pick their opponent, which the rating should not reward. Needs an rated flag on the invite path and the copy challenge for an unranked match.
  2. Coral text below 17px → --opp-text. The implementer excluded two selectors from the axe contrast rule: ledger words (14px) and value numerals on scored letters are coral at 3.4:1, while design system §9 allows coral text only at ≥17px. Decided: add one text-only token, --opp-text #C2402A (5.1:1 on paper), used wherever coral is text under 17px — ledger words, scored numerals, the profile's vs rows. Letters on the field, lanes, totals and squares stay --opp. This amends §2 to eight values and removes the exclusion.
  3. Numeral scale on phones → floor. Fixture B shows the design's own 18% value numeral at 6.5px in a 36px cell. Decided: max(9px, 18%), and hide the numeral below 32px cells; the letter's aria-label still carries the value.
